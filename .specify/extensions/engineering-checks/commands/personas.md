# Personas

For the item in `.specify/items/current.json` and its current spec and plan:

Who uses or is affected by this change. For each persona: who they are, what they are trying to do, and what this change means for them.

$ARGUMENTS

Write it to the item's record (`specs/<number>-<name>/item.md`) as a section headed `## Personas`. If something cannot be settled, write it under `## Questions` as `- Q-n <question> (for <who>) open`, and leave the section saying `open`.
